What UrbanOS is

Software that works out what the rules currently on record permit on a piece of land, with the drawing, and the government paper behind every number, before the builder spends his money.

It is an input to professional judgement, never a substitute for it, and it never produces a submission drawing.

A man has a plot, or is about to buy one. Today he asks someone and waits weeks. We want him to walk into his architect's office already knowing the shape of the answer.

How it is built, in three lines

The rulebook. Every rule stored with its instrument type, issuing office, date, which plots and areas it covers, what it supersedes, and whether it is in force or stayed. The software. Give it a plot and the rulebook, and it works out what is permitted, then draws it. The output. Drawings and a report where every number points back to the document it came from.

The rules never sit inside the AI. The AI reads documents and writes prose; the numbers live in the rulebook with their sources attached. No paper, no number.
